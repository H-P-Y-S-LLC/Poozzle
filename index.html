REAL-TIME PVP DUEL

Match a real player online, or send your room code and invite a friend. Fight them like a boss.

Your rival stands right below your board. Whoever drains the other's HP bar first wins the round.

Charge mana as you match, then unleash skills straight onto their board:

  • Random Freeze

    Freeze 5 random tiles on your rival's board — specials included — for 3 turns.

    80 mana
  • Random Blockers

    Convert 1–3 normal tiles into blockers from the PvP obstacle pool.

    99 mana
  • 🔄
    Weakness Shift

    Immediately reroll your rival's weakness color and throw their plan off.

    15 mana
  • 🌀
    Rolling Impact

    Clear all specials and reshuffle their board in one hit.

    80 mana

Win and you don't just take the rewards — loot their coins & gems straight from their pocket.

FIVE WAYS TO CLEAN HOUSE

Beyond PVP, four classic modes. Zero chill. Maximum satisfaction. Free. Offline.

⚔️
PVP Duel

Real-time duels. Online matchmaking or friend room codes. Drain their HP, loot their coins & gems.

🦗
Boss Brawl

Classic match-3 with teeth. 10 gimmick bosses, each with a unique weakness. Hit the weak spot, time your ultimate, watch that HP bar MELT.

💊
Vessel Run

A pill through blood vessel mazes. Slide to the bottom, collect germs, beat your best. Short. Satisfying. Repeat.

⬇️
Death Drop

Death line closing in from above. Match to drop. Heart pounding. Stay ahead or die — just react.

🏔️
Endless Ascent

Every board is procedurally generated. No two runs the same. Climb until you can't — your rank on the global leaderboard is your bragging right.

BUG DOSSIERS

Know your enemies

Earwig
THREAT PROFILE

The bathroom's warm-up tyrant. It may be the opening act, but it already knows how to turn a neat board into an awkward silence.

Spider
THREAT PROFILE

A control freak with eight legs and zero patience. Whenever the board looks too lively, it starts redecorating with fresh trouble.

Red Worm
THREAT PROFILE

A bad-tempered pest that likes every fight messy. If the board is not already unpleasant enough, it adds corrosion and locks down your best toys.

Sowbug
THREAT PROFILE

A tiny armored pessimist. It curls up, changes its weakness, and rolls through your plans like your confidence owed it money.

Moth Fly
THREAT PROFILE

A buzzing thief with the manners of a drainpipe goblin. It steals your rhythm and splashes puddles onto the board.

Mosquito
THREAT PROFILE

A high-pressure mosquito boss that dives onto tiles and infests them with larvae, forcing you to fight control and timing at once.

Gecko
THREAT PROFILE

A sticky-footed prowler that coats tiles with adhesive slime wherever it crawls. Every move makes the board messier.

Cockroach
THREAT PROFILE

The roach boss never fights fair. It freezes tiles and keeps returning with fresh minions like the worst encore in bathroom history.

White Ant
THREAT PROFILE

A white-ant foreman with a full moving crew. Hit it once, and the whole team starts hauling tiles off like a construction site.

Slug
THREAT PROFILE

A slow-moving strategist with a moss collection and a poisonous sense of humor. It drags fights until the board stops feeling like yours.
