Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. No Data Collection

MATCHFACE does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The game runs entirely in your browser — no accounts, no tracking, no analytics.

2. Local Only

All game state (score, settings, custom images you upload) is stored locally in your browser's localStorage or IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to any server.

3. Your Images Stay Yours

If you upload custom photos to replace game tiles, those images are used only on your device for rendering the board. They are never uploaded, shared, or accessible to us or any third party.

4. Third-Party Content

This website loads assets (tiles, icons, fonts) from CDN services (assets.poozzle.com, Google Fonts, Cloudflare). Those services may log standard HTTP request data (IP, user agent) per their own privacy policies. We have no control over their data practices.

5. Children's Privacy

MATCHFACE is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this page from time to time. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, reach out at onebelief@gmail.com.
